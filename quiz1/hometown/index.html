My Hometown

Tuban, Jawa Timur

Tuban is an abbreviation of “Batu Tiban,” which means “fallen rock.” Long ago, a large rock fell in my town, which is why my town is now called Tuban.



Tuban Regency is one of the 38 regencies and cities in the administrative region of East Java Province. Tuban Regency is located along the northern coast (Pantura) of Java. Tuban is known as the “City of Wali” because it is one of the cities in Java that served as a center for the spread of Islam; however, some people refer to it as the “city of tuak” because the Tuban region is famous for producing alcoholic beverages (tuak and legen) made from the sap of the siwalan palm (ental).
